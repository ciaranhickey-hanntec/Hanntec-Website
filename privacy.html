How Hanntec Engineering handles the details you send us.

Who we are

Hanntec Engineering Ltd, Fews, Kilmacthomas, Co. Waterford, X42 YH04. Email jack@hanntec.com.

What we collect and why

When you use the enquiry form we collect your name, email address, any phone number and company you give us, and your message. We use these only to reply to your enquiry and to take the conversation forward, including preparing a quotation.

Our legal basis

We process your details because you asked us to get in touch, and because it is in our legitimate interest to respond to business enquiries.

Who sees it

Your enquiry goes to our team by email. We do not sell your details or use them for marketing you did not ask for.

The enquiry form is run by Netlify, the company that hosts this website. Netlify stores each enquiry and emails it to us, acting only on our instructions. Netlify may handle data outside the European Economic Area, under safeguards approved by the European Commission. Our email provider acts on our instructions too.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them, and for a reasonable period after in case you get back in touch. Tell us if you want yours deleted sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, and you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie. Email us to make a request.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

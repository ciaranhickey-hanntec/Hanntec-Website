Services

Four disciplines, one team, from first drawing to final handover.

Design

From first sketch to issued-for-construction drawings, we design electrical and mechanical systems that are practical to install and easy to maintain.

  • M&E design and drawings
  • Process and pipework layouts
  • Surveys, feasibility and costing
  • Specifications and as-built records

Electrical installation & implementation

Registered electricians install and certify the system, then bring it into service. We implement the whole system, not just the cable.

  • Power distribution, lighting and containment
  • Fire detection and emergency lighting
  • Controls wiring and equipment connection
  • Data cabling, fibre and low-voltage systems
  • Testing, certification and handover

Electrical works certified by Safe Electric registered electricians.

Commissioning

We test every system against its specification, fix what we find, and document the result, so you take over something that works.

  • Functional and performance testing
  • Cable and fibre certification
  • Snagging and defect close-out
  • Handover packs and operator walk-throughs

Mechanical installation & process engineering

We install and modify plant, pipework and process equipment to specification, including live-plant work where downtime is not an option.

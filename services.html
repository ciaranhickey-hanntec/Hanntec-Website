Pre-project

Get the decision right before the money is spent.

ROI analysis & reporting

Before you commit capital, we put numbers on it. We model the cost, savings and payback of each option, and set it out in a report your board can act on.

  • Return on investment and payback models
  • Lifecycle cost and running-cost comparisons
  • Energy and economic modelling
  • Grant and funding assessments
  • Capital business cases and board-ready reports

Engineering advisory

Senior engineers on your side of the table. We help you define the project, weigh up the options and protect your interests, from first idea to final sign-off.

  • Feasibility and options studies
  • Project definition, budgets and specifications
  • Owner’s engineer: your representative through delivery
  • Risk and value management
  • Energy and sustainability advice
  • Asset management and maintenance strategy
  • Standards and compliance reviews

Electrical installation & implementation

Registered electricians install and certify the system, then bring it into service. We implement the whole system, not just the cable.

  • Power distribution, lighting and containment
  • Fire detection and emergency lighting
  • Controls wiring and equipment connection
  • Data cabling, fibre and low-voltage systems
  • Testing, certification and handover

Electrical works certified by Safe Electric registered electricians.

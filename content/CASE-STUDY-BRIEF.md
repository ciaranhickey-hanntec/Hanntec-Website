## 3. The story (one or two sentences each)

- **Summary:** what was built or improved, and the result.
- **The challenge:** what problem did the client have, and why did it matter to them? (cost, capacity, compliance, downtime, energy, deadline)
- **What we did:** 3 to 5 short points, e.g. "Modelled three heating options and their payback", "Designed the new plant room", "Installed and commissioned two heat pumps".
- **The result:** what changed for the client?

## 4. The numbers

Two to four figures make the case. Examples: project value or capex, payback period, energy or running-cost saving per year, downtime avoided, grant secured, programme met, floor area.

## 5. Photos

- Before and after from the same spot if possible (landscape, at least 1600 px wide)
- One or two of the finished work up close (plant room, panel, process line)
- Photos of the team on site are welcome. Check everyone shown is happy to appear.

## 6. Quote (optional)

A sentence from the client, with their name and role, if they're happy to be quoted.

About Hanntec

A multi-disciplinary engineering company in Kilmacthomas, Co. Waterford, working for industry across Ireland.

Engineers who see the job through.

Hanntec Engineering designs, installs and commissions electrical and mechanical systems. We manage the whole job, so you deal with one team from first drawing to handover.

Our business has grown by word of mouth and repeat work, because we take real care over what we build. We find solutions to the problems our clients have, and to the ones they didn’t think had a solution.

Core values

How we work, on every job.

  • Relationships

    Most of our work comes by recommendation. We keep it that way by looking after the people we work for.

  • Challenging design

    We question the brief and look for answers to problems our clients didn’t think had one.

  • Flexibility

    We are constantly evolving to meet our clients’ needs, and we are based to reach any site in Ireland.

  • Innovation

    Modern tools and methods, applied to practical, cost-effective results.

  • Safety first

    Every site, every task. Safe systems of work come before programme and price.

  • Sustainability

    Efficient systems and lower-carbon solutions, such as replacing fossil-fuel heating.
